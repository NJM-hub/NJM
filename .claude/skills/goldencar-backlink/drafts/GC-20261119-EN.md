## EN-MED (Medium)

### Title
Hiring a Private Car Service in Seoul: A Planning Guide for Business Visits and Special Trips

### Subtitle
What to decide before you book a car and driver in Seoul, from the airport run to the last dinner.

### Body
(cover image: EN_Private_Car_Service_Seoul_GOLDENCAR.png)

Most visitors to Seoul never think much about ground transport. The subway is fast, clean and easy to use, and taxis are everywhere. For a solo traveler or a couple with light bags, that is usually all you need.

Some trips are different. A visiting executive who lands in the evening and has meetings across the city the next morning. A delegation moving between a hotel, a factory and a dinner venue. Parents on their first trip abroad. A wedding party, an anniversary, a milestone birthday. On these trips, how people get from place to place shapes the whole experience, and a private car with a driver often makes more sense than piecing together trains and taxis.

This guide covers when a private car service is worth it, what to decide before you book, and how to plan the schedule so the car actually makes the day easier.

1. When a private car service makes sense

A private car service is not about luxury for its own sake. It solves specific problems:

- Visiting executives and partners. First impressions start at the airport. A planned pickup and a car that is ready for the next meeting says more than any welcome speech.
- Delegations and site visits. Groups move slowly. One car plan for the whole day keeps everyone together and on time.
- Family and special occasions. Older parents, young children, formal clothes or a lot of luggage all make public transport harder than it looks on a map.
- Packed schedules. Seoul is a big city. Moving between districts several times in one day is easier when you are not hunting for the next taxi.

If your trip fits one of these, it is worth planning transport as carefully as you plan the itinerary itself.

2. Decide what you actually need

Before you contact any provider, write down the basics. It saves time and avoids surprises on the day.

- Who is travelling: how many people, and whether anyone needs extra time or assistance.
- Luggage: suitcases, golf bags, samples or presentation materials all take space.
- How many cars: one car for everyone, or separate cars for different parts of the group.
- How long: just the airport run, one full day, or several days.
- What kind of occasion: a business visit, a family trip or a special event can each call for a different level of service.

Clear answers here make it much easier to get the right vehicle and the right plan.

It also helps to agree on who speaks for your group. One coordinator who holds the passenger list, flight details and any last-minute changes prevents the classic problem of three people sending three different updates. For business visits, this is usually someone on the host side; for family trips, the person who booked the hotels.

3. Build the schedule around the car

A car service works best when the schedule is written down in advance, not improvised from the back seat.

Airport arrival. Share the airport (Incheon Terminal 1, Terminal 2 or Gimpo), the flight number and the arrival time, so the pickup matches the actual landing.

Every stop, with addresses. List hotels, offices, restaurants and venues in order. Korean addresses are especially useful.

Buffers for traffic. Seoul traffic can be heavy at rush hour and on weekends. Leave room between appointments rather than planning back-to-back.

Evenings. Dinners often run late. Decide in advance how guests get back to the hotel.

Departure day. Count back from the flight time to set the pickup time, and remember that bags often multiply by the end of a trip.

Share the plan. Send the final schedule to everyone in the group, not just the coordinator. When guests know where they are going next and when the car will be waiting, the day runs calmly even if a meeting runs long.

4. Final checklist before you book

- Purpose of the trip: business visit, delegation, family trip or special occasion
- Number of passengers, including anyone who needs extra assistance
- Luggage and equipment
- Number of cars needed
- Arrival airport, terminal, flight number and time
- Full list of stops with addresses
- Time buffers for traffic
- Evening plans and the ride back to the hotel
- Departure day pickup time

A private car service in Seoul is most valuable when nobody has to think about it. Decide who is travelling, write down the full schedule, leave room for traffic, and the car becomes the quiet part of a trip that goes exactly as planned.

### Title
Renting a Car in Seoul? 5 Things to Check Before You Drive

### Body
Thinking about driving yourself around Korea?

A rental car can make trips outside Seoul easier, especially with family or a lot of luggage. Before you book, check:

- Your licence: many visitors need an International Driving Permit along with their home licence, so confirm what applies to you
- Pickup and return: where you collect the car and where you hand it back
- Navigation: plan how you will navigate, as map apps don't all offer the same driving directions in Korea
- Parking: check parking at your hotel and at the places you plan to visit
- Passengers and luggage: choose a car that fits everyone and every bag

Not keen on driving in a new city? A car with a driver or a private day tour is another option.
