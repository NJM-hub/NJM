## Title
One Free Day in Seoul? A Private Day Tour Checklist

## Body
Got one free day on your Seoul trip?

Before you plan a day out, check:
- How many people are going
- Where you want to be picked up
- What you're bringing (golf bags, ski gear, a stroller)
- Whether it's a day trip or an overnight stay
- How you'll get back to your hotel

Some trips are simply easier with your own car and driver, especially in a group, or in winter when you're carrying ski gear.
