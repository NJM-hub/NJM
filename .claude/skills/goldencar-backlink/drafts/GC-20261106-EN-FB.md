## Body
Landing at Incheon with kids and a mountain of luggage? 🧳

Before you book an Incheon airport pickup, check these three things:

1) Your arrival airport and terminal: Incheon T1, T2, or Gimpo
2) Passengers and bags (count strollers and golf bags too)
3) Waiting time: GOLDENCAR airport pickups include 60 minutes of free waiting from your reserved time

You can book the drop-off back to the airport at the same time.
