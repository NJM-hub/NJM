## Body
Hosting international guests in Seoul? The airport pickup is their first impression of your company.

Meetings, venues and dinners usually get planned weeks ahead. Ground transportation often gets booked last, even though it's the first thing your guests experience after a long flight.

Three things to confirm before your guests land:

1. The arrival airport and terminal. Seoul has two main airports, and Incheon has two terminals (T1 and T2). Check each guest's ticket, not just the flight number.

2. A buffer after landing. Immigration and baggage claim take time. Choose a service with a clear waiting policy. Our airport pickups at GOLDENCAR, for example, include 60 minutes of free waiting from the reserved time.

3. The full itinerary, shared early. Number of guests, number of bags, and any stops between the airport, the hotel and the office. Stops added on the day are harder to arrange.

For VIP guests, a dedicated vehicle with an attendant can make the whole visit feel organized from the first minute.
