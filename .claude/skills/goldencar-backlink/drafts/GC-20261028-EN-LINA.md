## Title
Seoul Airport Transfers for Business Travelers: What to Arrange Before Your Team Lands at Incheon

## Body
Flights get booked early. Hotels get booked early. Meeting rooms, dinners and site visits all get planned weeks in advance. Yet the trip from the airport to the hotel is often arranged last, sometimes on the day of arrival.

For a single traveler, that is usually fine. For a visiting team, a client delegation or a group attending an event in Seoul, it is where the first delays and the first impressions happen. This article covers what travel coordinators and business travelers should arrange before landing in Korea.

Know the airport and the terminal

Seoul is served by two main airports. Incheon International Airport is Korea's main international gateway, west of the city, with two passenger terminals: Terminal 1 (T1) and Terminal 2 (T2). Gimpo International Airport is inside Seoul and handles domestic flights and some regional international routes.

At Incheon, the arrival terminal depends on the airline. When several team members fly in on different carriers, they may land at different terminals on the same day. Check each ticket, not just the flight number, before you book a pickup.

Match the vehicle to people and luggage

Business trips rarely travel light. Sample cases, display materials, laptops and equipment cases add up quickly, and a group of four with luggage may not fit into one standard taxi.

When you book, give two numbers: passengers and bags. Include oversized items such as equipment cases or golf bags. It is the simplest way to make sure the right vehicle arrives.

Build a buffer after landing

Landing time is not exit time. Immigration and baggage claim can take longer than expected, especially during busy hours. Before you confirm any airport pickup in Seoul, ask how long the driver will wait.

At GOLDENCAR, for example, airport pickups include 60 minutes of free waiting from the reserved time.

Plan stops before the trip, not during it

Business itineraries often need a stop between the airport and the hotel: the office, a client's site or an event venue. Add these to the plan in advance rather than asking the driver on the day. With GOLDENCAR, additional stops are arranged through customer service before the trip.

Don't forget the return leg

Departure days are compressed. There is a checkout time, a last meeting and a flight to catch. Booking the drop-off to Incheon or Gimpo at the same time as the arrival pickup removes one more item from the final day.

When a standard transfer is not enough

For senior executives, VIP clients or official delegations, the arrival is part of the hosting experience. In those cases, consider a premium chauffeur service with a dedicated vehicle and attendant rather than a standard transfer. GOLDENCAR offers this as a separate premium protocol service in Seoul.

A checklist for travel coordinators

Before booking a Seoul airport transfer for a business trip, confirm:

- Arrival airport and terminal for each traveler (Incheon T1, Incheon T2 or Gimpo)
- Flight numbers, arrival dates and times
- Number of passengers and number of bags, including oversized items
- Full destination address, and any stops on the way
- The waiting-time policy
- Lead passenger name as shown on the passport, and a contact number
- Whether a return transfer to the airport is also needed

Ground transportation is a small part of a business trip, but it is the first part your travelers experience in Korea. A few minutes of planning before departure can make that first hour calm and predictable.
