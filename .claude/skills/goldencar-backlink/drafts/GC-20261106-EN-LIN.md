Hosting a delegation in Seoul? Plan the ground transport before the flights land.

A visiting delegation brings a lot of moving parts: several flights, different terminals, a packed schedule, and guests who expect everything to run on time.

Before booking a private driver in South Korea, three things are worth confirming:

1. Arrivals by terminal. Incheon has two terminals (T1 and T2), and some regional flights land at Gimpo. Group your guests by airport and terminal.

2. A realistic buffer. Immigration and baggage claim can run long. GOLDENCAR's airport pickups include 60 minutes of free waiting from the reserved time.

3. The full route. Office visits or venue stops between the airport and the hotel should be arranged in advance, not on the day.

For senior guests, a dedicated vehicle with an attendant turns the airport run into part of the hosting experience.
